Core Views

Democracy has always produced better reasoning than any other system. If we care about preserving that advantage, then AI safety is not optional infrastructure — it is quintessential.

Today, the road to AGI is split. One camp believes scale is sufficient (more compute, more data, faster iteration). Safety, in this view, is a downstream luxury. I think that is the easy path, and the wrong one. The harder path is to treat safety as a first-class scientific problem.

The existential risks emanating from AI (think concentration of power, secret loyalty, rogue systems) are bad news for the democracy-preserving world.

It is this purpose that drives me towards doing more honest science for AI safety.

Research

  • DeceptGuard: A Constitutional Oversight Framework for Detecting Deception in LLM Agents

    Mukhopadhyay, S.

    arXiv 2026 · Under peer review · Recommended by ARR to ACL Mains

    Paper / Code / Demo

    A constitutional oversight framework for deception detection in LLM agents using black-box, chain-of-thought, and activation-probe monitoring, with a scalable synthetic pipeline spanning a 12-category deception taxonomy; trained on 4.8K and evaluated on 9.2K execution-verified DeceptArena trajectories.

  • AMBEDKAR: A Multi-level Bias Elimination through a Decoding Approach with Knowledge Augmentation for Robust Constitutional Alignment of Language Models

    Mukhopadhyay, S., Kasat, A., Dubey, S., Karthikeyan, R., Sood, D., Jain, V., Chadha, A., & Das, A.

    arXiv 2025 · Featured in MIT Technology Review

    Paper / Code / Dataset / Project

    A first-of-its-kind inference-time constitutional alignment framework for LLMs that reinterprets speculative decoding as a fairness mechanism, introducing a new intervention axis at the generation-decoding locus alongside a comprehensive socio-cultural bias dataset; achieves up to 26.4% absolute bias reduction without retraining.

  • PAST2HARM: A Simple Adaptive Past Tense Attack for Jailbreaking Multimodal AI

    Mukhopadhyay, S.

    arXiv 2026

    Paper / Code

    A surprisingly simple adaptive jailbreak framework exposing a temporal generalization gap in multimodal safety fine-tuning, where past-tense reformulations achieve up to 100% attack success rate across frontier text-to-image models.
