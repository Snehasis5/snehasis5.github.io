Constitutional Alignment · Bias Mitigation · Inference-Time

AMBEDKAR: A Multi-level Bias Elimination through a Decoding Approach with Knowledge Augmentation for Robust Constitutional Alignment

S. Mukhopadhyay, A. Kasat, S. Dubey, R. Karthikeyan, et al.

Large language models exhibit strong caste- and religion-based biases in Indian contexts that Western-centric mitigation methods systematically fail to address. AMBEDKAR introduces a Constitution-Aware Decoding Layer at inference time — no retraining, no parameter updates. By reinterpreting speculative decoding as a fairness mechanism, it achieves up to 26.4% absolute bias reduction across 6 religions and 136 caste groups, evaluated against 14 frontier LLMs.

Biosafety · Adversarial Benchmarking · Dual-Use Risk

BioRiskBench: An Adversarial Benchmark for Dual-Use Risk of Biological Weapons

S. Mukhopadhyay et al.

Scaling frontier models embeds dual-use biological knowledge that persists despite high nominal refusal rates — showing capability and biosecurity compliance are not orthogonal. Using BioRiskBench-η and a novel cognitive-drift jailbreak, we demonstrate a ~90% attack success rate against aligned models. We argue that mitigating biological risk requires intrinsic latent unlearning, not post-hoc output filtering.

Deception Detection · Agentic Safety · Mechanistic Monitoring

DeceptGuard: A Constitutional Oversight Framework for Detecting Deception in LLM Agents

S. Mukhopadhyay et al.

Reliable detection of deceptive behavior in LLM agents is a prerequisite for safe deployment in high-stakes agentic contexts. Prior work on scheming detection relies exclusively on black-box monitors — discarding rich internal reasoning signals. DeceptGuard unifies three monitoring regimes and introduces DECEPTSYNTH, a scalable synthetic pipeline generating deception trajectories across a novel 12-category taxonomy spanning verbal, behavioral, and structural deception.

Regime I
Actions & Outputs Only
Observes only externally visible tool calls and agent outputs. The standard baseline used by most prior work on scheming detection — computationally cheap, but blind to covert planning that doesn't surface in behavior.
Regime II
Chain-of-Thought Reasoning Trace
Additionally observes the agent's CoT reasoning trace. Captures deceptive intent that manifests in intermediate reasoning steps before being sanitized from final outputs.
Regime III
Hidden-State Representations
Additionally reads hidden-state representations from a frozen open-weights encoder. Probes latent signals of deceptive planning — the most sensitive regime.
