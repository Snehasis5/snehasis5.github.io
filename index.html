I'm a final-year researcher at IIIT Kalyani (Electronics & Communication Engineering) and IIT Madras (Data Science & Applications), working on problems in AI safety and alignment.

My work spans deception detection in LLM agents, bias mitigation through decoding-time interventions, and jailbreak attacks on multimodal models. Research Philosophy

Robert Frost ends "The Road Not Taken" not with triumph, but with a quiet confession: the speaker doesn't actually know which road was less travelled — he only knows he will say it made all the difference. That gap between what we claim and what we can verify is precisely where my research lives.

Most AI safety failures don't announce themselves. A model can appear aligned under standard evaluation while encoding deceptive strategies, concealing information in latent activations, or exploiting temporal gaps in safety training. The danger is not the failure we can measure — it's the one we haven't built instruments to see yet.

My work tries to build those instruments. I'm drawn to intervention points that others tend to pass over: the decoding locus as a fairness lever, chain-of-thought traces as a window into agent intent, past-tense framing as a jailbreak surface. The less-travelled path in AI safety is often the one that reveals what was always there.

I believe safety and capability need not be in tension — but that conviction only holds if we take instrumentation seriously at every layer of the model stack.

    DeceptGuard: A Constitutional Oversight Framework for Detecting Deception in LLM Agents

    Mukhopadhyay, S.

    arXiv 2026 · Under peer review · Recommended by ARR to ACL Mains

    Paper / Code / Demo

    A constitutional oversight framework for deception detection in LLM agents using black-box, chain-of-thought, and activation-probe monitoring, with a scalable synthetic pipeline spanning a 12-category deception taxonomy; trained on 4.8K and evaluated on 9.2K execution-verified DeceptArena trajectories.

    AMBEDKAR: A Multi-level Bias Elimination through a Decoding Approach with Knowledge Augmentation for Robust Constitutional Alignment of Language Models

    Mukhopadhyay, S., Kasat, A., Dubey, S., Karthikeyan, R., Sood, D., Jain, V., Chadha, A., & Das, A.

    arXiv 2025 · Featured in MIT Technology Review

    Paper / Code / Dataset / Project

    A first-of-its-kind inference-time constitutional alignment framework for LLMs that reinterprets speculative decoding as a fairness mechanism, introducing a new intervention axis at the generation-decoding locus alongside a comprehensive socio-cultural bias dataset; achieves up to 26.4% absolute bias reduction without retraining.
